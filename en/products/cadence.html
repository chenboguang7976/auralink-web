What's different

One chord, four views — at the same time.

The slow part of learning chords isn't pressing the notes — it's knowing what you just played and where it sits. Cadence answers instantly, in realtime.

  • Instant chord namesA few notes and the chord name appears big — from triads to 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths and sus, plus alternate names and slash (inverted) chords.
  • Four views in syncChord (name + intervals), Piano (note-river), Fretboard (guitar neck) and Staff (notation) — switch tabs instantly; all describe the notes you're playing.
  • Notes flow like water on glassThe 88-key keyboard lights up under your hands and each note trails a glowing ribbon upward — easy to follow what's sounding, smooth, no flicker.
  • The shape on a real guitar neckThe chord appears on the fretboard with finger positions, root note and open strings; click the neck to cycle through other shapes.
  • It knows the key tooCadence tracks what you play and detects the key (major/minor), lighting the keys that belong to the scale — or lock a key yourself.
  • Use a keyboard or your DAWPlug a MIDI keyboard straight in; to analyse sound in a DAW, route it through loopMIDI (included in the download, with step-by-step instructions).

Not just the chord name — Cadence shows how to play it on the neck, with the root highlighted so you know where to start.

Click the neck to cycle through other shapes of the same chord.
