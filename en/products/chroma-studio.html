What sets it apart

Two plugins, one job: singing in key.

The slow part of tuning a vocal isn't turning knobs — it's knowing what key the song is in and staying in it all session. That is the part this pair takes off you.

  • Follows the key, no send buttonTurn on LiveKey Follow and Chroma Tune takes the key straight from Chroma Sense the moment the song changes — no stopping mid-session to set it by hand.
  • Finds the key while the music playsSense listens to the beat and gives you the key and the scale. Want more certainty? Let it analyse the whole file, or set the key yourself when you already know it.
  • From natural to full electronicSet RETUNE SPEED to 0 for the hard auto-tune effect; at 20–35 the vocal still sounds like a person. One knob decides the whole character.
  • Keeps the singer's slides and vibratoFLEX-TUNE leaves a note alone once it is close, HUMANIZE eases off on long held notes — the line never gets flattened into a machine.
  • Usable live2.3 ms of latency on every voice range — the singer hears themselves back with almost no delay, on stage or on stream.
  • Uses the song's real reference pitchWhile following Sense, Tune adopts the tuning the track is actually in — songs that were not tuned to 440 Hz still come out right.
Chroma Tune

Enough controls to shape it, not enough to get lost in.

Pitch meter

The note being sung and how far off it is in cents sit in the middle of the interface — needle at twelve o'clock means you are in tune.

Seven presets ready to go

Hard Tune, Modern Pop, Transparent, Barely There, Ballad, Robot and the default — step through them with two arrows instead of dialling it in again.

Your own presets

Save the settings you are using under a name. They stay on the machine and survive closing the DAW.

Tone, transpose, blend

Shift the formants to change vocal tone, transpose in semitones, and blend the untouched voice with the tuned one to taste.

Natural vibrato on top

Add vibrato with the shape and rate you choose, for long notes that come out too flat.

Set it to the voice

Soprano, alto / tenor, low male or an instrument — pick the right range and note tracking gets noticeably steadier.

Chroma Sense

The other half of the pair: the ear.

Put Sense on the beat bus and it listens, then settles on a key for the session. KEY LOCK decides how long a key is held before it may change, CONFIDENCE how sure it has to be before announcing one — together they stop the key jumping around in thin passages.

Chroma Sense — listen live, analyse a file up front, or set the key yourself.
Getting started

Install it, rescan, sing.

The installer puts both plugins in the standard VST3 folder — open your DAW, rescan, and Chroma Sense and Chroma Tune are in the list. To activate, hit License in the plugin, copy the Machine ID and send it to us for a key; one activation covers both plugins on that machine.
