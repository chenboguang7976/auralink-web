The awkward cases

Six places charts usually slip. Not here.

Charting software mostly agrees on ordinary cases and differs on just a few. These are those few.

  • Born after 11pm (晚子时)A birth between 23:00 and 23:59 already belongs to the next day for 日柱 and 时柱, labelled 早子 or 晚子.
  • Born on a 交节 dayThe turning moment is worked out to the minute, so someone born close to the boundary doesn't get the wrong 年柱 or 月柱.
  • Matches the almanac (节气)立春 2024 comes out at 16:27 — exactly as the astronomical almanac has it. Check any date you like against it.
  • Right for births far back in timeSomeone born centuries ago still gets the correct 日柱, undisturbed by the historical calendar reforms.
  • 真太阳时, if you want itOptionally correct for the longitude of the birthplace, for those who work that way — off by default, never forced on you.
  • 旺衰 you can argue with身强 or 身弱 is scored from 月令, 日支, 通根 and 生扶 — with a percentage — and 用神 follows from that score.
